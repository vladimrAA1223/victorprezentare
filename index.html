01 Focus · The buyers

Two buyers we can name, reach and afford to win

02 Feel · The insight

Their doubt is our brief: every objection needs a proof, not a claim

“It won't feel like a real AMG.”→The drive itself: seat time for every serious prospect.
“Charging takes too long here.”→10–80% in about 11 minutes at full power, shown live on our own roads.
“Will it hold its value?”→A leasing offer with a guaranteed future value, developed with the finance team.
“Is it practical enough?”→Five seats, a 607-litre boot and up to 700 km WLTP range.

“You need to have the possibility to feel it.”

Michael Schiebe, as Mercedes-AMG CEO, on selling the electric AMG GT, May 2026 (Motor1)

02 Feel · The channels

Four channels, chosen by how close they bring a buyer to the seat

  1. Owned data and personal invitationOwner records, AMG Private Lounge, an invitation from the dealer.
  2. Drive experiences48-hour home test drive; track days at Silesia Ring, Hungaroring, Slovakia Ring and MotorPark Romania.
  3. Earned media and creatorsFirst drives for leading motoring titles and YouTube creators in each market.
  4. Precision paid mediaYouTube and Instagram for configurator visitors; LinkedIn for business owners.

03 Fame · The idea

Five Capitals. 60 Hours.

One Mercedes-AMG GT SUV crosses all five markets to mark 60 years of AMG.

03 Fame · The idea

One car links five markets and proves charging on our own roads

How it works

  • June 2027, as the GT 55 and GT 63 arrive and AMG turns 60.
  • Each leg: a local owner and a local creator drive; a sceptic rides along.
  • Every charging stop is timed and published, honestly.
  • Finale in Bucharest, over the Transfăgărășan if it is open.

Why it fits

  • One car and one crew give five markets their own story.
  • Proof, not promise, on the roads our buyers drive.
  • It continues AMG's record run: 40,075 km in under 8 days.

Distances are approximate road distances. Concept AMG GT XX record: Mercedes-AMG, Nardò, August 2025.

Targets: set per market after wave 1 gives a baseline. No reliable public benchmark exists for test-drive conversion in this class.

Priorities

With limited resources, I would protect the seat and cut the reach

1

Protect first

  • Sales-team training
  • Personal invitations to owners
  • 48-hour home test drives
2

Then fund

  • Five Capitals, 60 Hours
  • First drives for media and creators
3

Only if budget allows

  • Paid reach beyond retargeting
  • Lifestyle sponsorships
  • Motor-show stand

What I would not do: television, or five separate national campaigns. They buy awareness, and awareness is not our problem. Doubt is.
